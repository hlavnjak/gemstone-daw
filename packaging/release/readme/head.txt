ReSynth Studio @VERSION@ — pre-release
==========================================

A small DAW that turns any recording (.wav / .mp3 / .m4a) into a playable,
editable additive-synthesis instrument through its embedded LeSynth Fourier
plugin, and hosts VST3, CLAP, VST2 and LV2 plugins.

Source, issues and documentation: https://github.com/hlavnjak/resynth-studio
