Third-party software in ReSynth Studio
========================================================================

ReSynth Studio and the LeSynth Fourier plugin embedded in it
(internal_plugins/) are Copyright 2026 Jakub Hlavnicka and licensed under the
Apache License 2.0 (see LICENSE).

They are built from the following open-source Rust crates, each under its own
licence, reproduced below. Two points need attention:

* GPLv3: the embedded LeSynth Fourier plugin (liblesynth_fourier.so /
  lesynth_fourier.dll / liblesynth_fourier.dylib) exports its VST3 interface
  through vst3-sys, which is licensed under the GNU GPL version 3. That plugin
  binary is therefore distributed under the terms of the GPLv3 (the Apache-2.0
  code in it is GPLv3-compatible). Its complete corresponding source code is
  public:
    https://github.com/hlavnjak/lesynth-fourier
    https://github.com/hlavnjak/nih-plug (branch reduce_egui_high_cpu_consumption)
    https://github.com/hlavnjak/baseview (branch reactive-idle-wait)
    https://github.com/hlavnjak/egui-baseview (branch reactive-idle-wait)
    https://github.com/robbert-vdh/vst3-sys (branch fix/drop-box-from-raw)
  with the exact revisions pinned in lesynth-fourier's Cargo.lock.

* MPL-2.0: the symphonia crates (audio decoding) are used unmodified; their
  source is available from https://github.com/pdeljanov/Symphonia and
  crates.io.

ReSynth Studio's own source: https://github.com/hlavnjak/resynth-studio
